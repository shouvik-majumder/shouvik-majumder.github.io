Projects

Papers in mechanistic interpretability and neural representation geometry that I have reimplemented, and a small analysis package. Code is on GitHub.

Belief state geometry in transformers

I trained small transformers on hidden Markov processes (Mess3 and RRXOR) and used linear probes to test whether the Bayesian belief state over hidden states is linearly represented in the residual stream, following Shai et al. (NeurIPS 2024).

Code · Paper

Circular features for days and months

I reproduced the circular representations of weekdays and months in GPT-2 and Gemma-2 reported by Engels et al. (ICLR 2025), including the rotation interventions on modular arithmetic prompts.

Code · Paper

Manifold capacity

I implemented the mean-field manifold capacity estimator of Chung, Lee and Sompolinsky (PRX 2018) in NumPy and SciPy and validated it against closed-form results and direct linear-separability simulations on synthetic manifolds.

Code · Paper

Steering resistance

I steered Gemma-2 models with Gemma Scope sparse autoencoder directions and measured how often the model returns to the original question under steering, following McKenzie et al. (ICML 2026).

Code · Paper

Neuralgeom

A Python package I am developing for geometric analysis of neural population trajectories: pullback metrics, subspace distances along a trajectory, persistent homology, and fixed-point analysis of recurrent networks.
